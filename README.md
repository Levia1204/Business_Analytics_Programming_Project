
# Business Services Industry Analysis

**Financial Benchmarking & NLP Analysis | Python | UBC Master of Business Analytics**

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the U.S. Business Services industry (SIC Major Group 73) through financial benchmarking and natural language processing of corporate disclosures.

The analysis combines financial records from 1994–2025 with 2020 and 2025 SEC 10-K business disclosures to evaluate industry performance, identify changes in disclosure language, and assess Microsoft's financial strategy.

## Key Findings

- Analyzed 30,610 firm-year observations across 3,273 companies in the Business Services sector.
- Examined stock prices, sales performance, geographical distribution, and return on assets (ROA).
- Applied text cleaning, word frequency analysis, TF-IDF, and Word2Vec to compare corporate disclosures from 2020 and 2025.
- Found that Microsoft's revenue grew at a 14.52% CAGR from 2020 to 2025, while its ROA declined from 19.94% in 2022 to 16.45% in 2025.
- Proposed milestone-based funding reviews to support capital allocation and asset productivity.

## Tools & Techniques

**Python:** pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, NLTK, scikit-learn, Gensim, WordCloud

**Methods:** Data cleaning, financial benchmarking, descriptive analytics, text mining, TF-IDF, word embeddings, and data visualization.
